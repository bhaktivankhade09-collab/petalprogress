## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines technical productivity, algorithm mastery, and data structures problem-solving through a calming, feminine, and botanical lens. DSA and interview preparation are traditionally associated with cold, high-stress, gamified, or stark interfaces; this system transforms that journey into a quiet, nurturing botanical sanctuary where solved problems bloom like watercolor petals and progress feels restorative rather than exhausting.

The visual style blends soft minimalism with ethereal tactile accents: crisp porcelain-white cards, gossamer petal borders, diffused rosy luminescence, and sparkling milestones. Soft botanical motifs (tulips, lilies, floating blossom textures) provide visual respite between dense code logic and algorithmic complexity. The UI conveys grace, intellectual clarity, emotional safety, and mindful achievement.

## Colors

The palette establishes a high-contrast yet soothing sanctuary environment centered around floral porcelain and deep tea-rose accents:

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base canvas uses soft blush porcelain (`#FFF8FA`), creating a warm paper-like foundation. Surfaces, panels, and modal containers use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to maintain optical separation, bordered by gossamer rose hairline dividers (`#F0E1E7`).
- **Primary & Interactive:** Deep Rose (`#874D63`) serves as the primary visual anchor for high-priority actions, active tabs, and key navigational landmarks, delivering WCAG AAA contrast against white and blush surfaces. Accent Pink (`#D88CA6`) and Soft Pink (`#F5DCE5`) provide gentle tinting, active state fills, focus halos, and glow reflections.
- **Typography:** Primary text is grounded in deep plum charcoal (`#46363E`), avoiding harsh cold black while preserving crisp readability for code blocks and analytical explanations. Muted metadata and helper hints use warm mauve slate (`#927E86`).
- **Status & Milestones:** Algorithmic success, accepted submissions, and completed milestones bloom with Floral Green (`#6E9075`) paired with soft sage chip fills (`#D4E2D4`). High-streak achievements, star ratings, and celebratory milestones gleam in luminous Gold Sparkle (`#E2B053`).

## Typography

Typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans throughout, offering friendly geometric curves, immaculate humanist legibility, and refined optical balance.

- **Headlines:** Set with slightly tightened letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to produce an intimate, editorial presence. Headers establish quiet authority without visual harshness.
- **Body & Code Notes:** Rendered with open line heights (`1.5` to `1.6x`) to facilitate focused reading during algorithm walkthroughs and editorial study notes.
- **Micro-labels & Badges:** Use subtle uppercase or title case with gentle letter spacing (`0.01em` to `0.03em`), providing crisp legibility on problem category pills (e.g., "DYNAMIC PROGRAMMING", "BINARY SEARCH").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous whitespace and tranquil breathing room, counteracting the anxiety often sparked by tracking technical problem lists.

- **Grid Architecture:** A flexible 12-column grid spans desktop views (max-width `1280px`), collapsing into 8 columns on tablet viewports and 4 columns on mobile handsets.
- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop containers utilize a comfortable `2rem` outer padding with `1.25rem` gutters, scaling gracefully down to `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters on compact displays.
- **Component Breathing Space:** Cards, analytical charts, and LeetCode problem trackers maintain generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`), ensuring metrics, status tags, and complexity notation remain decoupled and serene.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth mimics delicate watercolor paper sheets layered over morning light, avoiding heavy artificial drops in favor of rose-tinted ambient glows:

- **Base Level (Flush):** Flat panels rely strictly on hairline borders (`1px solid #F0E1E7`) over the `#FFF8FA` blush foundation.
- **Floating Surfaces & Cards:** Problem cards, streak counters, and solution dialogs use a signature rose-tinted bloom shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(216, 140, 166, 0.12)`.
- **Hover & Active Blooming:** Interactive cards lift slightly on pointer interaction (`translateY(-2px)`), deepening the radiance to `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(216, 140, 166, 0.20)`.
- **Modal Overlays & Popovers:** Elevated sheets combine a translucent glass backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with `rgba(255, 248, 250, 0.75)` and an ambient shadow of `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(135, 77, 99, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system evokes organic flower petals and polished river pebbles:

- **Surfaces & Cards:** Standard interface containers use a soft `0.5rem` to `1rem` corner radius, creating tactile, friendly edges that soften technical data.
- **Pills & Badges:** Tags, difficulty indicators (Easy, Medium, Hard), status badges, and primary action buttons utilize full circular pill curvature (`9999px` or `rounded-full`), mirroring soft tulip contours.
- **Delicate Line Treatment:** Dividers and card frames consistently utilize an ultra-fine `1px` stroke in `#F0E1E7`, preserving a crisp, premium stationery feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Bloom") Button:** Filled with Deep Rose (`#874D63`), white text, full pill radius (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover, applies an ambient rose halo (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(216, 140, 166, 0.35)`) and gentle lightness shift.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background with `1px solid #D88CA6`, colored with `#874D63`. On hover, fills with Soft Pink (`#F5DCE5` at 50% opacity).
- **Celebration / Milestone Action:** Accent gold-tinted subtle border with floral green indicator for completed problem sessions.

### Problem Chips & Badges
- **Difficulty Pills:**
  - *Easy (Sprout):* Background `#D4E2D4`, text `#425F48`, pill shape.
  - *Medium (Blossom):* Background `#F5DCE5`, text `#874D63`, pill shape.
  - *Hard (Deep Orchid):* Background `#EED2DE`, text `#5C283E`, pill shape.
- **Status Badges:** Small circular icon dot or petal symbol accompanied by uppercase label (`label-sm`).

### Cards & Problem Trackers
- Encased in pure white `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #F0E1E7` outline and the signature soft glow shadow.
- Interior layout separates algorithmic taxonomy tags, acceptance rates, and completion checkboxes with clean hairline dividers.

### Form Inputs & Code Search
- Inset field with `#FFFFFF` or `#FFF8FA` background, `1px solid #F0E1E7` border, rounded-lg geometry, and `#46363E` text.
- Focus state activates an Accent Pink glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(216, 140, 166, 0.25)` with border shifting to `#D88CA6`.

### Checkboxes & Progress Toggles
- Custom circular or rounded-square checkboxes. Unchecked: `1.5px solid #D88CA6` with white fill. Checked: solid `#6E9075` (Floral Green) or `#874D63` (Deep Rose) with a crisp white checkmark blossom glyph.

### Streak & Milestone Trackers
- Circular blossom rings where each resolved LeetCode problem fills a single watercolor petal, culminating in a gold sparkle glow (`#E2B053`) when daily goals are reached.
